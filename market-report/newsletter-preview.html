Our Q3 Windsor-Essex market report is ready. You can read it below.

Industrial sales volume fell 59% from Q2. That’s a big drop, but Q2 included a single $30M sale. That one transaction accounted for more than half the quarter’s total.

Q3 had five industrial sales totalling $18.3M. It’s worth keeping that earlier sale in mind when comparing the two quarters.

Multi-family went the other way.

Multi-family sales totalled $6.1M, up 117% from Q2. That was three sales covering 49 units. All three buildings sold below the trailing 12-month median price per unit, so the jump in volume doesn’t mean prices rose with it.
