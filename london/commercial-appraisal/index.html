Different assets. Different evidence.

The property shapes
the analysis.

A downtown office building, a Wellington Road retail plaza and an industrial property near Highway 401 need different comparisons. We consider the location, use and available evidence for your property.

01
Office & retail
Leases, tenant mix, vacancy, operating costs and comparable sales. From professional offices to neighbourhood plazas and mixed-use buildings.
02
Industrial & warehouse
Clear height, loading, building condition, site utility and access, alongside income and sales evidence for comparable industrial assets.
03
Multi-residential investment
Rent rolls, tenancy, operating expenses and building condition. The analysis considers the income a purchaser can reasonably expect.
04
Land & special-purpose property
Permitted use, servicing, site characteristics and the limits of available comparable evidence. Scope and specialist requirements are agreed first.

Before you commission an appraisal

Five things that
keep the work

You do not need a complete file to make the first enquiry. Start with what you know. We will identify the information the assignment needs.

  1. The property

    Address, current use, approximate building and site size, and any known changes or constraints.

  2. The decision

    Financing, purchase, sale, reporting or a legal matter. Tell us who needs to rely on the report and the relevant valuation date.

  3. The lender's instructions

    For financing, confirm the lender's approved appraisers and report requirements. Paying for a report does not automatically make it suitable for another lender.

  4. The supporting information

    For income-producing property: rent roll, leases and recent operating statements. Plans, surveys, tax information and prior reports may also help.

  5. The deadline

    Share the date and any conditions attached to it. Fees and delivery depend on scope, access, complexity and information availability.

Which valuation method applies?

Income analysis considers rental income and operating costs. Direct comparison considers comparable sales. Cost analysis considers land value and depreciated improvements. The appraiser selects and weighs the approaches that suit the property and assignment, rather than applying the same formula to every asset.

Who provides commercial property appraisals in London, Ontario?

Metrix Realty Group provides independent commercial appraisals from its London office. The team includes AACI-designated professionals. We confirm assignment suitability and the responsible appraiser after reviewing the property, purpose and intended users. Meet our team.

Will my lender accept the appraisal?

Acceptance depends on your lender's approved appraisers, report requirements and the assignment. Confirm these before ordering and share the lender's instructions with us. We cannot guarantee acceptance by every lender.

How much does a commercial appraisal cost?

There is no single fee for every commercial property. Property type, complexity, purpose, scope, access and supporting information affect the work required. We confirm the fee after discussing your assignment.

How long will the appraisal take?

Delivery depends on scope, property access, available information and current capacity. Tell us your required date at the first enquiry so we can confirm availability and agree on realistic timing.

What does the AACI designation tell me?

AACI is the Accredited Appraiser Canadian Institute designation. It covers a broad range of real property appraisal assignments. Check the individual appraiser's credentials and experience for your property and intended use. Read our AACI guide.

Can I reuse a report for a different lender or purpose?

Do not assume so. The report is prepared for an agreed purpose and authorized users. Contact the appraiser if these change. Compare bank and private appraisals.

Do you work outside London?

Metrix has offices in London and Windsor and serves Southwestern Ontario. Coverage and availability depend on the property and assignment. For Windsor-Essex commercial work, visit our Windsor commercial appraisal page.
