A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Where does Metrix work?

Our offices are in London and Windsor. We serve Southwestern Ontario, with assignment coverage confirmed for the property location and type. Explore our London and Windsor services.

What should I have ready when I enquire?

Share the property address, property type, reason for the appraisal, intended users and your deadline. For financing, include your lender's requirements. We will confirm the scope and information needed.

Will my lender accept the report?

Lender requirements vary. Confirm the lender's approved appraisers and report requirements before ordering. Tell us the intended lender so we can discuss suitability before proceeding.

How much does an appraisal cost, and how long does it take?

Fees and timing depend on the property, complexity, scope and available information. We confirm a quote and realistic delivery date after reviewing your requirements.

Can I book a residential appraisal in Windsor?

Standard residential appraisal requests in Windsor and Essex County are not accepted through our booking form. For complex residential assignments, contact our office to discuss suitability and availability.
